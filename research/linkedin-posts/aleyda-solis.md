How do you measure visibility in the age of AI Search (AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini)?

You can't rely on traditional organic click-through rate (CTR) curves anymore.
Here is the 3-layer framework I recommend to all my clients:

1. **Presence/Share of Voice:** How often does your brand appear in AI-generated answers for your target keyword clusters?
2. **Readiness/Sources:** Are your key informational pages being cited as the sources for those answers?
3. **Business Impact:** Are those citations driving qualified traffic, or are users finding the answer directly inside the AI interface and leaving?

I've put together a full AI Search Optimization Checklist to help you audit and execute this strategy. Let's adapt our tracking to the new reality!

## Post 2
**Date:** May 18, 2026

If your team is struggling to get high-quality outputs from AI models for SEO tasks, the issue is likely your prompt structure.

Stop sending vague prompts like "write an SEO article about X."
Instead, use the "5Ws and the H" framework to structure your prompts:

- **What:** The exact task (e.g., generate 5 title tag variations).
- **Where:** The platform or context (e.g., for a SaaS homepage).
- **Who:** The target audience persona.
- **Why:** The business goal or search intent.
- **When:** The time frame or freshness requirements.
- **How:** The format, constraints, and length.

Detailed prompting saves hours of human editing time. Invest in prompt engineering for your SEO workflows!

## Post 3
**Date:** April 28, 2026

AI will not replace SEOs, but SEOs who use AI will replace those who don't.

We can use AI to automate:
- Schema markup generation
- XML sitemap troubleshooting
- Initial keyword clustering
- Meta tag creation at scale

But we must NEVER automate:
- Editorial judgment
- Strategic prioritization
- Brand voice and unique data insertion

Use AI to handle the manual, repetitive tasks so you can spend your time on what really moves the needle: strategy, user research, and brand positioning.
