## Post 1
**Date:** June 7, 2026

If your SaaS SEO strategy is still focused on writing 2,000-word guides to "rank for keywords," you are burning budget.

AI can write a better, cleaner, faster definition of "what is CRM software" than you can.
So why would Google or users click on your article?
They won't.

We've shifted our entire focus at AI Search Mastery to "narrative-led content."
Use AI to do the research, map out the outlines, and organize the semantic entities.
But have an actual human practitioner write the case study, insert real screenshots of your product, and explain the "opinionated workflow" of how to solve the problem.

Optimize for the practitioner, not the crawler.

## Post 2
**Date:** May 20, 2026

Here is how we use Claude to speed up our content production by 80% without losing quality:

1. We don't ask it to write the article.
2. We feed it our client's unique customer interview transcripts and product documentation.
3. We ask it to extract the key customer pain points, specific language (jargon) they use, and use cases.
4. We use those extracted insights to outline the content structure.
5. A human writer writes the draft based on that hyper-targeted outline.

AI is a researcher and analyst, not a writer. Stop asking it to do creative work and start asking it to do analytical work.

## Post 3
**Date:** April 22, 2026

The future of SaaS SEO is "Search Market Fit."

Most SaaS companies have hundreds of pages that rank for keywords but drive zero revenue.
In the AI search era, broad informational queries will be answered directly by the LLM (zero-click search).

You need to align your content strategy only with the queries that are closest to buying intent, and then use your product as the answer to the searcher's problem.

Stop measuring impressions and traffic. Measure demo signups, trial starts, and product-led conversions from organic search.
If your SEO team is still talking about "search volume," they are optimizing for vanity, not revenue.
