## Post 1
**Date:** June 8, 2026

Most companies are approaching AI Search (AEO) all wrong. They think it's just "SEO 2.0."

They are trying to optimize for keywords and track rankings in ChatGPT or Gemini.
This is a trap.

In a world of conversational answers, there is no page 1. There is no ranked list. There is only "the answer" and the references.

Instead of asking "how do we rank for this keyword?", you should be asking:
1. Is our brand recognized by the LLM as the authority on this topic?
2. Are third-party sites validating our expertise in their own content (which the LLM trains on)?
3. Does our content solve a specific, deep user query, rather than chasing broad volume?

If you're still reporting on keyword rankings to your board, you're measuring the past. Start measuring Brand Influence and Entity Authority. That's the future of organic growth.

## Post 2
**Date:** May 24, 2026

The traditional SEO role is dead.

The era of writing Jira tickets, fixing meta descriptions, and pulling basic audit reports from SEMrush is coming to a close.
AI can write the descriptions. AI can find the broken links.

If you want to survive as an SEO professional in the next 3 years, you need to transition into a Product Manager.

Product-Led SEO is about creating search experiences that help users solve problems. It means working with engineers, understanding product analytics, and building assets that AI engines can't easily replicate or summarize on their own.

SEOs who act like engineers or copywriters will be replaced by AI. SEOs who act like Product Managers will be irreplaceable.

## Post 3
**Date:** April 15, 2026

We are finally moving out of the "AI content hype" phase and into the reality check phase.

Over the last two years, I've seen dozens of sites try to build programmatic AI engines to publish 10,000 articles a month.
Almost all of them saw a temporary spike followed by a massive drop in Google's recent core updates.

Why? Because Google's helpful content systems are looking for E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness.

Raw, unedited AI content has zero experience and zero original insight. It is just a mashup of what already exists on the web.

If you want to use AI to scale, use it to help you research, structure, and edit. But let humans bring the original data, case studies, and opinions.
