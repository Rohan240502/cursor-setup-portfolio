## Post 1
**Date:** June 1, 2026

Is AI Overview traffic a zero-sum game?

In our latest analysis of enterprise SaaS sites, we found that search intent is bifurcating.
For informational, top-of-funnel searches ("how to run an agile meeting"), click-through rates (CTR) from Google have dropped by 35% because AI Overviews answer the query directly.

But for high-intent, bottom-of-funnel searches ("best agile project management software"), AI Overviews actually increase click volume to the featured brands, acting as a recommendations engine.

This means you must stop writing generic guidebooks. Your organic growth strategy needs to focus on product-led, comparison, and review content that forces AI engines to list you as a recommended solution.

## Post 2
**Date:** May 15, 2026

The biggest mistake enterprise brands make with AI content is scaling volume instead of depth.

I've seen companies spin up tools to generate 1,000 keyword-optimized landing pages. That's a relic of 2018 SEO.
Instead, use AI to run a gap analysis between your site and your top competitors.

At Shopify and Atlassian, we focused on "Topical Authority." AI models can ingest your entire page library and identify exactly which sub-topics you are missing to complete the semantic network of a topic.

Scale your topical coverage, not your page count. That's how you win in 2026.

## Post 3
**Date:** April 18, 2026

AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity are trained on the web—but they don't value all content equally.

They look for brand consensus and external trust signals.
If 50 different authoritative industry blogs mention your brand as a leader in "customer support automation," LLMs will cite you as the answer to "what is the best customer support automation tool?"

Your "SEO" strategy is now inseparable from PR, brand building, and digital footprint management.
If nobody is talking about you off-site, no amount of on-page optimization will get you cited in AI Search.
