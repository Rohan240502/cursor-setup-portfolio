## Post 1
**Date:** June 3, 2026

Everyone is talking about how AI lets you write a blog post in 10 seconds.
What they aren't telling you is that publishing raw AI content is the fastest way to get penalized by Google.

At Content Distribution, we scale content production to hundreds of articles a month—but we don't let AI write them.

We use automation to handle candidate sourcing, testing, and onboarding (via Workello) and keyword grouping (via Cluster AI).
But the actual content? Written by human writers, edited by human editors, and polished by subject matter experts.

If you want to win in organic search, you need content velocity *and* quality. AI can help you manage the systems, but humans must write the copy.

## Post 2
**Date:** May 14, 2026

The biggest bottleneck in scaling content operations is hiring writers.
Most companies waste 90% of their time reading bad resumes and portfolios.

When we built our agency's writing team, we realized that resumes are useless.
Instead, we built a testing funnel:

1. Candidate applies.
2. They are automatically sent a 500-word writing test.
3. Only the candidates who pass the test are reviewed by an editor.

This funnel (which we productized into Workello) allowed us to hire 30 high-quality writers in a month.

Build a system for your people, and the content velocity will take care of itself.

## Post 3
**Date:** April 19, 2026

If you want to rank in organic search, you need "Topical Authority."
Google ranks sites that cover a topic in exhaustive detail, not sites that write one-off posts.

But how do you know which topics to cover?
You cluster.

We use Cluster AI to group thousands of keywords into unique pages, ensuring that we never write duplicate or cannibalized content.

AI is excellent at grouping data and mapping structure. Use it to build your roadmap, and then hire human experts to write the content. That's the winning combo.
