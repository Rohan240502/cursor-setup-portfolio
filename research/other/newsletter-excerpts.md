### Issue: State of AI Search Optimization 2026
**Date:** January 14, 2026

> "Traditional SEO remains foundational, but AI visibility demands different content strategies: Conversational query coverage matters more than head-term rankings. External validation matters more than owned content. Structure matters more than keyword density. Brands that build systematic optimization programs now will compound advantages as LLM traffic scales. The shift from ranked lists to definitive answers is irreversible."

### Issue: Topic-First SEO
**Date:** July 2025

> "Optimizing for topics (not just keywords) is the most overlooked mindset shift in SEO. Keyword-first SEO creates surface-level content and cannibalization. By switching to a topic-first mindset, teams map out the semantic coverage first, and then write pages that cover the entire landscape, preventing duplicate content and improving topical authority score."
