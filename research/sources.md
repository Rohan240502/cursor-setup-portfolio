# Research Methodology: AI-Powered SEO Content Production

## What's in this repo
The playbook at `/playbook/ai-seo-content-production-playbook.md` is built from live web research into 10 practitioners actively working on AI-powered SEO/content production. Every claim in the playbook is tied to a real, resolvable source link and, where available, a real publish date.
